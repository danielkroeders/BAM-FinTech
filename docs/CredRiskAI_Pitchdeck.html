CredRisk.AI Underwriter Workbench 01

The problem

SME underwriting is too manual for the risk hiding inside the file.

Analysts spend time collecting context before they can judge risk. Documents, financial ratios, fraud signals, portfolio exposure, and final decisions often live in separate tools.

Slow triage

High-risk cases wait in the same queue as clean applications.

Low transparency

Analysts need to explain why a score moved, not just see a number.

Weak governance

Model output, AI advice, and human final decisions must stay separate.

Problem worth solving 02

The solution

One workbench for scoring, explaining, reviewing, and monitoring SME loan applications.

1

2

3

Review

4

Monitor

Queues, portfolio risk, and audit history keep operations visible.

Product promise 03

Why this wins

CredRisk.AI gives lenders speed without losing control.

Operational speed

Assigned tasks, workboard filters, bulk rejection, and case handoff reduce review friction.

Explainable confidence

Risk drivers translate raw model signals into analyst-readable rationale.

Human accountability

The tool recommends; Ms. Cooper records the final analyst action separately.

AI where useful

Hosted or local LLMs can challenge the baseline with a qualitative second review.

Bank-grade audit

Session decisions, memo generation, and audit history support governance.

Scalable foundation

The MVP can connect to PSD2, accounting, registry, KYB, and document pipelines.

Differentiation 04

Architecture
